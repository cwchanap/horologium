## Player-facing outcome

After mastering Ceres, the player should encounter a fifth world that is unmistakably **Io**: sulfur-yellow volcanic terrain with orange lava heat and charcoal basalt, not another Mars-like rust planet or Ceres-like grey-blue ice world. Its three sites should remain visually distinguishable both as Site Deck thumbnails and as the native-size tiled Mine Site background; each deposit should be legible above that background.

This is **art only**. HPA-262 wires the images and implements the planet, progression, save contract and integration tests after HPA-87 merges. No animated nodes or new renderer abstractions.

## Consistency and composition rules

- Keep the existing illustrative mining visual language: coherent perspective, controlled highlights, rich material contrast and consistent outline softness. Do not change the camera or invent a second art style.
- Across three caverns, preserve camera height, wall/floor proportion, horizon/orientation, open-center mining space and scale. They repeat at native 800×1200 with `BoxFit.none` and `ImageRepeat.repeat` in a 50×50 × 56 px (2800×2800) Mine Site; seams occur at x=800/1600/2400 and y=1200/2400. Keep the opposing edges sufficiently close for a smooth wrap (mean absolute RGB difference ≤12 for first/last columns and first/last rows — a target derived from the tighter cobalt-family caverns, not the whole shipped set: `iridium.png` measures 12.32 and `silica.png` 12.03); avoid tiny rooms and busy grid-like marks. The numeric check does not prove invisible joins: directional strata, wall motifs and features clipped at an edge (e.g. a steam wisp exiting the top row) can still read as seams, so inspect the actual repeated tile and keep strong features off the border rows/columns. The owner accepted the delivered set's remaining visible joins as a documented exception to the smooth-wrap goal; further reduction stays desirable but is not a merge blocker for HPA-87.
- Caverns are fully opaque **RGB** scenes (not transparent node plates). On **360×640 and 430×932 portrait** screens, `_PortraitSiteDeck` uses each cavern as its site-card image with `BoxFit.cover` and fixed first/second/third card heights **216 / 170 / 104 px**, at card widths **332 / 402 px** respectively (14 px side insets). These are the **outer card sizes**: the 1–1.5 px `Border.all` draws inside the card bounds, so the visible art frame is ~1–2 px smaller on each side. These heights do not vary with unlock state, but the card has **three** art treatments: **operational** (gradient `Color.fromRGBO(6,10,16,.05)/.5/.93`), **unlocked but not operational** (the darker `.15/.58/.94` gradient — the darkest non-locked state), and **locked** (0.48 image opacity plus a flat `Color(0x70060A10)` overlay). Inspect the actual centered crop under all three; the short 104 px third card is especially important for Osmium. The **128 / 148 / 196 px** heights belong to the *landscape* `_SiteCard` branch and should only be used for a separately identified landscape preview. Preserve readable sulfur/cinnabar/osmium material on both side walls **within source y≈400–800** as a directional composition guide, not a fixed crop boundary; judge the resulting material visibility in the real portrait crops.
- A second, easily missed cavern render path: `MiningSheetScene` paints the active site's `cavernAsset` as a **full-screen `BoxFit.cover` background** at **0.5 opacity** (0.42 for Settings) behind the Technology and Settings bottom sheets, under a dark `Color(0x99..0xF5)` gradient. On a 360×640 portrait this shows only ~676 of 800 source px; on 430×932 only ~553 px — side-wall minerals are largely cropped, so identifying material must also read in the central floor band.
- Nodes are **single isolated deposits** on genuine alpha, with similar margins, bottom anchor and contact shadow; no scenery, lettering, UI, multiple scattered deposits, shadows in opaque square backgrounds or baked checkerboard. For the alpha >16 bounding box (left/top/right/bottom, right/bottom exclusive), target the **newer static-node envelope** measured from the cobalt-family generation: left 40–61, top 4–19, right 454–475, bottom 495–509 (cobalt: 61/10/459/502). This is a target, not a shipped average — the older `coal.png` (28/26/486/486) and `stone.png` (20/24/493/481) fall outside it. These bounds keep `BoxFit.contain` deposits from rendering noticeably smaller than their peers. Check at **22 px** Site Deck chip, **30 px** Stellar Map icon **only when unlocked** (locked sites render a lock-only branch, not a faded node), and **80/120/168 px** Mine Site sizes (**0.62 opacity when unsurveyed**).
- The planet is a single isolated globe on alpha; preserve Mars' framing and lighting, with Io's warm volcanic palette. Its alpha >16 bbox should fit the Homeworld/Lunar/Mars 640 px planet envelope: left 17–26, top 13–25, right 614–624, bottom 607–620. It renders at **four** sizes, not one: **34 px** in the Site Deck header, and on the Stellar Map **172 px** on the active card (positioned `right: -27, top: -43` inside `clipBehavior: Clip.antiAlias`, so the **top-right limb is clipped away**), **96 px** on unlocked non-active cards (`right: -8`), and **92 px at 0.34 opacity** on the locked teaser (`right: -2, top: -2`). The volcanic plume is Io's one distinguishing feature — place it on a limb that survives the active-card clip (left or lower-left, not top-right). No labels, border, black square or scene backdrop.
- Use the HPA-262 silhouette fallback accents as material guides: sulfur = `Colors.yellowAccent`, cinnabar = `Colors.redAccent`, osmium = `Color(0xFF9FB4C7)`. These are color-direction anchors, not a demand for flat-colored art. **Compare against all 12 shipped caverns and nodes**, not only cobalt: `caverns/gold.png` is already amber/yellow, `caverns/iron_ore.png` red and `caverns/silica.png` orange. Separate sulfur from gold by steaming sulfur crust, crystal habit and charcoal basalt rather than hue alone; make cinnabar's cooled lava channels distinguish it from iron.
- **Reject and re-edit** baked transparency checkerboard in the Io planet and osmium node; no automatic normalization of either because grey/white mineral highlights can be mistaken for the checkerboard. Prefer re-edit for all assets. If edge-connected checkerboard normalization is unavoidable for another eligible image, limit it to proven exterior pixels only, capture the **changed-pixel mask and count** in the PR, and recheck the alpha bbox afterward. Never recolor all checkerboard-like pixels or erase interior mineral detail. Inspect all RGBA assets on dark and light backgrounds.

## Acceptance / explicit review gate

1. Seven exact paths, no extras. Each PNG decodes at its target dimensions and mode: planet **640×640 RGBA**, three caverns **800×1200 RGB**, three deposits **512×512 RGBA**.
2. Verify alpha >16 bounding boxes against the shipped node/planet reference envelopes and compare first/last cavern rows and columns (mean absolute RGB difference ≤12). Reject/re-edit failures rather than weakening the checks by default; inspect every alpha edge on dark and light backgrounds. Any permitted checkerboard cleanup must disclose its changed-pixel mask/count and still meet these checks.
3. Review a labeled **seven-image output contact sheet plus the 12 existing caverns and 12 existing nodes** for palette context. Include native-scale repeats of each cavern and **portrait** Site Deck cards at both 360×640 and 430×932 using the actual **216/170/104 px outer card heights** (art frame ~1–2 px inside the border), corresponding frame widths and **all three state treatments**: operational, unlocked-not-operational (darker gradient), and locked (**0.48 image opacity** plus flat overlay). Preview nodes over matching caverns at **22, 26, 30, 80, 120 and 168 px** (26 px is the offline-return sheet icon; 30 px Stellar Map node **unlocked only**; **0.62 unsurveyed Mine Site opacity**), each cavern as the **0.5-opacity `MiningSheetScene` sheet background** on both portrait sizes, and the planet at **34, 92 (0.34 opacity), 96 and 172 px** (172 px includes the active card's clipped top-right bleed). In card crops, confirm the middle-band side-wall minerals remain visible after the normal dark UI gradient. Store previews and raw generations in project files, **not** the repository.
4. Approve the pilot and finished style against Mars, cobalt cavern/node and the shipped gold/iron/silica palette neighbours. Confirm material identity and legibility at mobile size, especially locked osmium.
5. **Owner explicitly approves the full seven-image set on this PR before merge.** Do not mark HPA-87 complete before that approval and the asset commit.

## Out of scope

Io content definitions and economics, save or controller changes, Planet/Mining Site view wiring, animated node frame families, new shaders, atlases, image-generation tooling, SFX, and changes to `pubspec.yaml`. The existing manifest already includes `planets/`, `caverns/` and `nodes/`.
